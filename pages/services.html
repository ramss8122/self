Common Questions About Our Services

Everything you need to know before placing your cake order.

How far in advance should I book?

For custom cakes and wedding orders, we recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance. Same-day options are available for selected designs and smaller orders.

Can I request a flavour combination?

Yes. We can mix flavours, fillings and finishes to create a cake that matches your vision and event perfectly.

Absolutely. We offer fresh delivery, setup support and safe handling for birthdays, weddings, office events and private gatherings.
