Cake Storage, Slicing & Leftovers
Buttercream / Truffle Cakes: Store in an air-conditioned room (18°C–22°C) or refrigerator. Bring out to room temperature 20 to 30 minutes before cutting for the creamiest texture.
Fresh Fruit / Whipped Cream: Keep refrigerated (2°C–6°C) until cutting.
Fondant Sculpted Cakes: Keep in a cool, dry air-conditioned room. Avoid humid domestic refrigerators as condensation can melt sugar figurines.
Dip a tall chef's knife in hot water for 5 seconds, wipe the blade dry with a clean kitchen towel, and slice straight down in one smooth stroke. Wipe and reheat the blade between each cut. This prevents ganache from dragging and yields razor-sharp bakery slices!
Leftovers stay fresh in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. You can also wrap individual slices tightly in cling film and foil to freeze for up to 30 days. Thaw overnight in the fridge before enjoying.
