Our Beginning

Baked with Heart
Since 2012

Our bakery was born in 2012 when Executive Chef Meera Krishnan started baking custom birthday cakes from her home kitchen in Coimbatore. Armed with a French Pastry diploma from Le Cordon Bleu, she laid the foundation of craftsmanship and passion.

In 2018, Head Pastry Chef Karthik Raja joined the lead, bringing expertise from Paris and revolutionizing our gourmet artisan menu with signature sourdoughs and delicate choux pastries. Today, our 18-member culinary team crafts over 200 custom creations monthly.

  • ✓ Fresh ingredients sourced locally every morning
  • ✓ No artificial flavours or preservatives
  • ✓ Fully custom designs from sketch to sugar art
  • ✓ Same-day delivery across Coimbatore
