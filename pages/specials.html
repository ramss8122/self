Chef's Specials FAQs

Common questions about ordering and storing our limited-edition creations.

Why are specials produced in limited quantities? +

Our Chef's Specials rely on fresh seasonal harvest crops (like Ratnagiri Alphonso mangoes or Italian Bronte pistachios) and artisanal labor-intensive techniques. We bake in small batches of 20-30 cakes per week to ensure 100% peak freshness.

Can Chef's Specials be made 100% eggless? +

Yes! All our seasonal specials (including the Lotus Biscoff, Alphonso Mango, and Pistachio Raspberry) are available in our signature 100% eggless recipe without compromising any moisture or fluffiness.

What is the shelf life and storage recommendation? +

Keep refrigerated at 4°C to 8°C. For maximum flavor and silky ganache texture, take the cake out of the refrigerator 15–20 minutes before serving. Best enjoyed within 48 to 72 hours of delivery.

Can I customize the message or add gift packaging? +

Yes! Every order includes a complimentary custom embossed message plaque, premium cake box, birthday sparkler, and a personalized handwritten message card.
