Our Beginning

Baked with Heart
Since 2012

Our bakery was born in 2012 when Chef Meera Krishnan started baking custom birthday cakes from her home kitchen in Coimbatore. Armed with a French Pastry diploma from Le Cordon Bleu and an obsession with fresh ingredients, she created cakes that were as beautiful as they were delicious.

Word spread quickly. By 2015, the bakery moved to a dedicated bakehouse. Today, with a team of 18 passionate bakers and cake artists, we create over 200 custom cakes every month — each one a unique story told in sugar, cream and butter.

  • ✓ Fresh ingredients sourced locally every morning
  • ✓ No artificial flavours or preservatives
  • ✓ Fully custom designs from sketch to sugar art
  • ✓ Same-day delivery across Coimbatore
