How much in advance should I place my cake order? ▼
For standard signature cakes and bento boxes, we accept orders just 2 to 4 hours in advance! For multi-tier designer wedding cakes or custom 3D fondant sculptures, we recommend placing your order 2 to 4 days ahead.
Do you offer 100% Eggless, Vegan, and Gluten-Free cakes? ▼
Yes! Over 95% of our cake flavors can be prepared 100% Eggless with the exact same melt-in-your-mouth fluffiness. We also bake certified Vegan, Gluten-Free, and Sugar-Free/Keto cakes upon request.
How are the cakes delivered safely without damage? ▼
All cakes are dispatched in specialized shock-absorbing, climate-controlled delivery boxes with dedicated riders to ensure your cake arrives in picture-perfect condition.
Can I customize the flavor, size, and message on the cake? ▼
Absolutely! You can choose from over 20+ sponge and frosting flavors, select any weight from 0.5kg to 10kg+, and add any customized message or photo topper via our online booking form.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible delivery locations.
