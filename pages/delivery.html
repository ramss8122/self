Baker's Best Practices

Cake Care & Storage Instructions

Follow these quick tips to keep your cake tasting and looking bakery-fresh.

❄️

Buttercream & Truffle Cakes

Refrigerate upon delivery. Bring to room temperature 20 to 30 minutes before cutting for the smoothest, melt-in-the-mouth texture.

🍓

Fresh Fruit & Whipped Cream

Must be kept chilled at all times (2°C – 6°C). Serve chilled immediately upon cutting. Consume within 48 hours of delivery.

🎨

3D Fondant Sculpted Cakes

Store in an air-conditioned room (not inside a high-humidity fridge) to prevent sugar condensation and preserve structural crispness.
