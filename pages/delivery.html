Baker's Best Practices

Cake Care, Storage & Slicing Masterclass

Follow these expert guidelines from our master chefs to keep your cake looking and tasting fresh.

Self-Transport by Car

Always place the cake box on the flat floorboard of your car (passenger side) with AC blowing down on maximum. NEVER place the cake on a slanted car seat or hold it tilted on your lap.

Safe Unboxing Technique

Our boxes feature easy-tear front flaps. Pull the side perforations down and slide the cake board horizontally outward. Never try to lift a heavy cake upward over tall box edges.

Buttercream & Truffle Storage

Refrigerate upon delivery. Bring out to room temperature 20 to 30 minutes before cutting so the butter ganache softens into its signature silky, melt-in-the-mouth texture.

Fresh Fruit & Fresh Cream Care

Fresh dairy cream cakes must remain strictly refrigerated (2°C – 6°C) until the moment of cutting. Do not leave exposed under hot halogen event lighting or direct sunlight.

3D Fondant Sculpted Cakes

Store in an air-conditioned room (18°C–22°C). Avoid standard domestic refrigerators, as opening the door causes humidity condensation that can soften sugar craft figurines.

Chef's Knife Slicing Secret

Dip your slicing knife in a tall mug of warm water for 5 seconds, wipe dry, and cut straight down. Reheat and wipe the blade after each slice for pristine, bakery-grade presentation slices.

Perfect Serving Temperature

Buttercream and ganache cakes taste best at room temperature. Take your cake out of the refrigerator 30 to 45 minutes before slicing to allow the flavors to soften and reach their rich, creamy texture.

Safe Car Transport Rule

When carrying the cake in a vehicle, always place the box flat on the car floorboard (passenger side) rather than on a tilted seat. Keep the AC running on high to maintain a cool environment during transit.
