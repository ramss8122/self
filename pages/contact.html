✦ Quick Answers ✦

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a custom cake? ▼
For standard custom cakes, we recommend at least 48 hours' notice. For wedding or multi-tier cakes, we suggest booking 2–4 weeks in advance to allow time for design consultation and preparation.
Do you offer same-day delivery? ▼
Yes! We offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 12 PM from our standard menu. Custom cakes may not be available same-day. WhatsApp us for urgent enquiries.
Can I request a specific flavour not on the menu? ▼
Absolutely! We love experimenting. Contact us and describe your ideal flavour — our development chef will work to create something special for you.
Do you cater for dietary requirements (vegan, eggless, gluten-free)? ▼
Yes, we offer eggless cakes across our entire menu at no extra charge. Vegan and gluten-free options are available on request — please mention your requirements when ordering.
What is your cancellation and refund policy? ▼
Orders cancelled 24+ hours before delivery receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are eligible for a 50% store credit. Custom cakes with completed design work are non-refundable once work has begun.
