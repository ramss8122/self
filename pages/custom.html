How much advance notice do I need to give for a custom cake? +

We recommend booking standard 3D & custom cakes 48 to 72 hours in advance. For intricate multi-tier cakes, heavy hand-sculpted fondant figurines, or gravity-defying designs, please allow 4 to 7 days so our sugar artists can sculpt and dry the delicate elements properly. Rush orders can be accommodated subject to chef availability.

Can I provide a photo reference from Pinterest or Instagram? +

Yes! Over 90% of our custom orders start from a customer's Pinterest board, Instagram reel, invitation card color palette, or kid's favorite toy. You can upload the images directly on our booking form or share them over WhatsApp with our chef.

Are all 3D sculptures and decorations 100% edible? +

All our fondant figurines, edible photo prints, sugar flowers, and geode crystals are made using 100% food-grade sugar paste and certified edible colors. For tall 3D structures or gravity-defying cakes, we use internal food-safe support dowels (which are easily removed before serving).

Can 2-tier or multi-tier cakes have different flavors on each tier? +

Absolutely! You can choose different sponge flavors and gourmet ganache fillings for every tier at no extra charge. For example, Bottom Tier: Belgian Dark Cocoa Ganache, Top Tier: Alphonso Mango Velvet.

How do you guarantee safe delivery for delicate 3D cakes? +

We use specialized heavy-duty cake bases, anti-slip transit mats, and custom shock-absorbing cake boxes. All delicate custom and multi-tier deliveries are dispatched in air-conditioned four-wheelers with a dedicated handler, backed by our 100% Transit Protection Guarantee.
